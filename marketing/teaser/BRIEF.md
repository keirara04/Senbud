## Intent

Official beta teaser for Senbud, a student money buddy app. Unnarrated, motion-first,
sleek After Effects feel: kinetic type, masked reveals, count-ups, whips, punch-ins.
Kind, plain voice from the landing page. Ends on "Beta now open".

## Customizations

- Auto-fill beat: input types "lunch 12, grab 8", splits into two receipt rows (Food 12, Transport 8).
- Count-up on "Safe to spend today" hero number (highlighter yellow on cobalt panel).
- "Can I afford it?" beat: types 40, answer card "Doable, but tight".
- Rapid triplet: No bank password / Free plan forever / Your data stays on your device.
- End card: logo stroke draw + highlighter swipe, wordmark "Senbud", "Beta now open", shimmer sweep.
- SFX only: whooshes, ticks, typing clicks. No music.

## Notes

- No emoji anywhere.
- Stationery look: dotted-notebook paper, cobalt pen, yellow highlighter, thermal receipt.
- One yellow thing per scene, at most one cobalt panel per scene.
- Brand ease cubic-bezier(0.22, 0.68, 0.14, 1).
- System fonts (SF Pro). Tabular figures for amounts.
- Never `hyperframes publish`; render locally.
