The screens we haven’t built yet

Eight screens for the Expo app, drawn at real size (1 px = 1 pt on a 375 pt iPhone) from the tokens in @senbud/core/tokens. Example data is a student in Petaling Jaya on a RM 1,400 month with RM 450 rent. Flip the theme at the top right to check every frame in dark mode.

  • One cobalt panelIt holds the screen’s answer. Everything else is a white card on paper.
  • One highlighterOnly the key number is yellow. If two things are yellow, neither stands out.
  • Words carry statusA colour never says “over” alone. No red panels, no scolding.
  • 48 pt taps, system fontsSF Pro / Roboto, tabular figures for every amount, native sheets and alerts.
Applies to every screen

Navigation

Today the app is a stack with text links. Eight screens need a home base.

  • Five-slot tab bar: Home, Budgets, Bud, Friends, Treats. The four sides are places you return to. Bud sits raised in the middle on a 56 pt cobalt circle; tapping it opens the assistant sheet over whatever screen you’re on, it doesn’t switch tabs. Built with Expo Router’s JS tabs and a custom centre button (native tabs can’t raise one), SF Symbols for the other four.
  • Pushed from a tab, not tabs themselves: Activity (from the pace card), Can I afford it? (from Home), Wrapped (from the Home nudge on the 1st and from Budgets → Past recaps), Settings (button at the top right of Home).
  • No floating button anywhere. Bud in the tab bar is the assistant on every tab, Home included, so nothing covers the last row of a list. Pushed screens keep the tab bar; full-screen modals (Wrapped, paywall, sheets) hide it.
  • Large titles on tab roots (Budgets, Friends, Treats). Home has no title; the date sits where it would be, and the safe-to-spend panel is the first thing you see.
The assistant’s face

Meet Bud

A little thermal receipt with a face. The Highlighter-S tile stays the app’s logo; Bud is who you talk to.

  • Made from the brand’s own parts: receipt-white body with torn edges, ink outline, two printed lines for a fringe, coral blush. No highlighter on Bud, so it never competes with a screen’s key number.
  • Four faces, each with a job: happy (default, the tab bar), thinking (an answer is loading), oops (offline or AI off) and yay (Pro unlocked, a jar goal reached).
  • Where it shows: the middle of the tab bar (38 pt inside a raised 56 pt cobalt circle), the assistant sheet header, the welcome screen, and empty states. Never inside copy, and it doesn’t replace words.
  • Decorative for screen readers. The button keeps its label “Ask Senbud”. A small idle wobble on the button turns off with Reduce Motion.
  • Same drawing in light and dark: the white body reads on paper, night and cobalt.

Screen 0 · first launch, before Setup

Welcome and sign in

Say what Senbud is in one breath, then get out of the way.

  • There’s no login today, on purpose. The spec promises no bank login and no accounts, and Pro works through an anonymous RevenueCat id. So the first screen is a welcome, not a sign-in wall: “Get started” goes straight to Setup.
  • Returning people tap “I’ve used Senbud before” and get the two things that bring them back: restore a backup file, and restore Pro.
  • Decided: no accounts now. Sign in with Apple or Google comes later as a Pro feature, only for encrypted sync between phones. It’s optional and skippable. If Google sign-in is offered on iOS, Sign in with Apple must be too (App Store rule 4.8), and it goes first.
  • Bud, the logo and one cobalt button. No highlighter except the logo’s own “bud”.
Screen 1 · update to a built screen

Home v2

How much can I spend today, and is anything coming up?

  • Hero stays put: the cobalt panel with safe-to-spend in highlighter. Nothing else on Home is yellow.
  • Today’s nudge (spec 5) is one card under the rent and pace cards. Rent and pace nudges merge into their own card instead of repeating it in a second card, so the separate nudge card only carries priorities 3–5: renewals, old IOUs, treats.
  • Quick-capture bar is pinned above the tab bar: a camera button, the text field and a pen button for adding by hand. Real build uses SF Symbols (camera, square.and.pencil), with labels for VoiceOver.
  • The text field is also the assistant’s door on Home. “kopi 4.50 grab 12” saves straight away with Undo (local parsing, works with AI off). A question (“can I afford…”) opens the assistant sheet with the answer. Bud in the tab bar opens the same sheet empty.
  • The pen button opens a sheet: amount keypad first, then category chips, with favourites and “repeat last”, as spec 4.1 says.
Screen 2 · tab

Budgets

Where is the month’s money going, and what’s already spoken for?

  • Hero: cobalt panel with what’s left this month in highlighter, and the overall bar on the panel (new Progress tone="panel": white 22% track; mint, amber or coralLight fill, since plain coral fails on cobalt).
  • Categories as a stamp card ledger on a thermal receipt: name, dotted leader, “248 / 400”, then 10 stamps (one per 10% of the limit) in the category’s ink, and how much is left. Close turns the words amber; over adds a coral stamp outside the card and “12 over”. Rows with no limit show the amount and “No limit”.
  • Rent and bills as a grouped list with due dates. Detected subscriptions ask first (“Is Spotify a subscription?”), as spec 4.10 says. Detection is Pro.
  • Edit sits in the nav bar; tapping a row opens a small sheet with one Field. “Until payday” and more than one budget show a Pro lock.
Screen 3 · pushed from Home

Can I afford it?

Type a price, get a straight answer before you pay.

  • Price first: one big Field with the numeric keyboard, an optional “What is it?”, and your treats’ last prices as one-tap picks.
  • Hero: the cobalt panel answers. Verdict in words (“Yes!”, “Doable, but tight.”, “Not this month.”), the key number in highlighter, hours of work under a hairline.
  • “No” never turns red. The panel stays cobalt and the highlighter shows when it fits, which is something to do rather than a scolding.
  • No hourly rate yet: hours are swapped for one line that offers to add it. Allowance-only students can ignore it.
  • The answer updates as you type (debounced), so there’s no “Check” button. “I bought it” saves it as spent with Undo.
Screen 4 · tab

Friends

Who owes who, and an easy nudge to settle up.

  • Hero: the net balance on the cobalt panel, in highlighter both ways. The words say the direction (“owed to you” / “you owe overall”), so “you owe” isn’t painted coral. This changes the section 4 table, which said good/bad colour.
  • One row per friend with initials (no contacts access, no photos), the latest note and date, and the amount with “owes you” in good ink or “you owe” in plain dim text.
  • IOUs older than 7 days show “Remind” right on the row, which opens the friend sheet. “Send on WhatsApp” opens WhatsApp with the message already written; you pick the chat and tap send, so Senbud still never sends anything itself and never needs contacts. If WhatsApp isn’t installed, that button is hidden and “Other apps” becomes the main one. “Other apps” opens the share sheet (Telegram, Messages); “Copy” copies it. “Mark paid” has Undo.
  • Split is three short steps instead of one crowded grid: 1) who was there, who paid, by item or evenly; 2) pick a person, then tap their items on the receipt (tabs show running totals; “1 item left” until every item has an owner); 3) check everyone’s share and save. Evenly skips step 2. By item is Pro.
Screen 5 · tab

Treats

Guilt-free treats, paid for by leftover budget.

  • Hero: a jar that fills. Drawn on the cobalt panel with the highlighter as the money inside, so the fill is the key number, with the total in highlighter under it. Coins show through the yellow; tick marks at quarters; a dashed line at the goal.
  • A jar needs a size, so it gets a goal: “Movie night for two, RM 60”. Fill = jar ÷ goal. No goal yet: an empty jar and “Set a goal”. Goal reached: it overflows a little and the button spends it. This is a new field (goalCents on the treat jar).
  • “Add to jar” goes inside the panel. The yellow button sits next to the yellow number, so the screen still has one yellow zone. The Marker on the jar name is dropped; it would be a second yellow.
  • Treat rows show your own last price, “every N days”, and a status in words (“Fits today”, “Next in 9 days”). Prices come from your history, never guessed.
  • Month-end leftover is offered as a sheet on the 1st (or from the Home nudge): move it to the jar or keep it in the budget.
  • History is a short list under the treats; “See all” pushes the full log.
Screen 6 · full-screen story, later phase

Wrapped

A month of spending as a story worth sharing.

  • Full-bleed cobalt cards, one stat per card, with progress segments at the top. Tap right/left to move, swipe down to close. No autoplay: you go at your own pace, and Reduce Motion turns transitions into cuts.
  • Cobalt and the white receipt don’t change with the theme, so Wrapped looks the same in light and dark (like the web’s panels), and the share image does too.
  • Category card: a bar chart on a white receipt. Bar fills are the category ink colours, which pass 3:1 on white. On a dark surface they’d fail (about 2:1), so any chart drawn on surface in dark mode uses the category tints instead.
  • Share image: 9:16 thermal receipt with torn edges, no amounts by default (percentages and fun facts only). A switch shows amounts. Uses the native share sheet.
  • Pro: free gets the basic monthly cards; Pro adds the full set and the year-end edition.
Screen 7 · pushed from the Home gear

Settings

Change the basics, see what leaves the phone, and leave cleanly.

  • No hero. iOS-style inset grouped rows (new ListRow), in this order: Money, AI, Look, Your data, Senbud Pro, then Erase on its own at the bottom.
  • AI is one native Switch with a plain line under it, and a “What gets sent” row that pushes the full explanation, reusing the receipt consent text word for word.
  • Theme is a Segmented Light / Dark / System (closes the open item in section 7).
  • Pro is a card with one line of what you get, “See plans”, which opens the paywall (concept in 7b; code in pro.tsx on the Pro branch) and “Restore purchases” as a TextButton. Pro members see “Senbud Pro · Active” and “Manage”.
  • Erase all data is a danger button with the native alert; the red button says “Erase” and the body says what’s lost.
Screen 7b · full-screen modal

Senbud Pro paywall

Show what Pro does, pick a plan, pay. Nothing pushy.

  • Opened from Settings → “See plans”, any ProLock chip, and the camera button for free users. A modal with Close at the top right and no tab bar.
  • Copy is the one you chose: “Senbud Pro does the typing for you”, three benefits (snap receipts, unlimited assistant, subscription reminders), yearly preselected with its monthly price, and small print with Terms, Privacy and Restore. No trial wording, no ratings, no countdowns.
  • The selected plan is the cobalt panel. Plans are a radio group; tapping the other one moves the panel there. The price on the panel is white, not highlighter, because the one yellow thing is the buy button (highlight), and that stays true in dark mode.
  • The button says what you pay: “Subscribe for $39 a year”. While the store sheet is up it shows a spinner and “Waiting for the App Store”.
  • Prices come from RevenueCat in the person’s own store currency. Shown here in USD as configured ($4.99 a month, $39 a year = $3.25 a month).
Screen 8 · from Bud in the tab bar, phase 2

Assistant

Ask or add in plain words. It should feel like talking to Bud, not a chat app.

  • Bud prints its answers. Bud is a receipt, so replies come out as small torn-edge receipt slips in mono type: a header (“Can I afford it?”, “Added”, “Split this?”), dotted-leader lines, a total, then actions under the slip. Your messages stay short cobalt notes on the right. Nobody mistakes it for WhatsApp.
  • Notebook paper behind the chat (the dotted background from the web, now that react-native-svg is in), and Bud’s face beside each slip shows its state: happy, thinking, oops.
  • Opening it isn’t an empty chat. Bud says “What’s up?” and offers four starter cards for the things it does best. Tapping one fills the box.
  • One marked number per slip with the highlighter stroke, like the share image. The screen behind is dimmed, so it doesn’t compete with that screen’s hero.
  • Rules decide, AI writes the words. Can I afford it uses screen 3’s verdict; quick adds save with Undo on the slip; unsure splits ask first. AI off and offline still take quick adds.
Proposed additions to mobile/src/ui.tsx

New components

What these screens need that the kit doesn’t have yet. Native first; no new UI libraries.

  • Each one takes colours from useTheme(), keeps 48 pt taps and gets a row in the section 3 table when it’s built.
  • Only one needs a new dependency: ReceiptCard (torn edges) needs react-native-svg, which section 7 already deferred until Wrapped.

Tabs (native)

Expo Router tabs with SF Symbols: house, chart.bar, person.2, gift. Tint = accent. OS sizes, labels and Dynamic Type. Material icons on Android.

NudgeCard

Props: message, up to 2 actions, onDismiss. A surface card with a “Today” label, one sentence, text buttons. Max one per day (core picks it). Not a live region: it’s there on load, not an alert.

CaptureBar

Pinned above the tab bar on Home; rises with the keyboard. Camera button, text field, pen button (48 pt each, labelled “Snap a receipt”, “Add by hand”).

  • Text with amounts goes to parseSpending and shows UndoBar.
  • A question opens AssistantSheet with it.
  • Camera without Pro opens the Pro screen.

Sheet

Use the native form sheet (presentation: 'formSheet', detents medium/large), not a JS sheet library. surface fill, radius.lg, grabber, shadow (it floats). Used for Add by hand, friend, leftover, assistant.

AmountPad

Big amount display plus a 3×4 keypad, keys ≥ 48 pt tall. Hides the decimal key for zero-decimal currencies (KRW, JPY). Each key is a button with its digit as the label; ⌫ is “Delete”. Haptic tick on press.

ListRow / List

Inset grouped list. Row: label, optional value (dim), chevron or Switch. Min 52 pt tall, hairline separators inset 16. Wraps at large text: the value drops under the label.

Switch

React Native Switch, trackColor.true = accent (cobalt light, highlighter dark), thumbColor = surface. Thumb on track: 7.6:1 light, 15:1 dark.

Progress tone="panel"

For bars on the cobalt hero. Track is white at 22%. Fills: mint (4.0:1 on cobalt), amber (4.7:1), and coralLight for over, since plain coral is 2.7:1. Still always paired with words.

PersonTabs + ClaimRow

Split step 2. Tabs (52 pt, running totals) pick a person; full-width 52 pt rows on the receipt claim items for them, with a checkbox and the owners’ initials. Unassigned rows are hatched amber. Replaces the old grid of per-item circles.

TreatJar

The Treats hero. A jar on cobalt that fills with highlighter toward a goal; coins show through, ticks at quarters, dashed goal line, a little overflow when full. Needs a jar goal (goalCents).

Bud

The assistant mascot in four faces (happy, thinking, oops, yay). Decorative, same in both themes, never in copy. Lives in the middle of the tab bar, the sheet header, Welcome and empty states.

BudTab

The centre slot of the tab bar: a 56 pt panel circle raised 22 pt above the bar with Bud inside, label “Bud” under it, float shadow, dark-mode onPanelDim ring. It opens the assistant sheet instead of switching tabs (tabPress → preventDefault). Role button, label “Ask Bud”. Idle wobble off under Reduce Motion.

StoryPager

Horizontal pager of full-bleed panel pages with segment bars. Tap halves to move, swipe down to close. No autoplay. Reduce Motion: cuts, no slide. Each page is one accessibility element read as a sentence.

BarChart

Horizontal bars, max 6, sorted, one per category, with the % as text on every bar (never colour alone). Fills: category ink on white or receipt; category tint on dark surface (12–15:1 vs 2:1 for inks).

Before anything is coded

Open questions

Each one changes what gets built.

  1. Friends hero in highlighter both ways? Section 4 said good/bad colour. Painting “you owe RM 8” coral reads as a telling-off.
  2. Drop the Marker on Treats? Highlighter number + Marker + Add-to-jar would be three yellow things. Bigger version of the same problem: in dark mode accent is highlighter, so every primary button is yellow. Friends (“Split a bill”, “Mark paid”) and the assistant’s “Yes, split it” end up with two yellow things. Give dark primary its own fill?
  3. Are category limits free or Pro? The spec says they “exist already” but makes “multiple budgets” Pro.
  4. What does a chat question send? The Settings frame proposes “question + short summary, never the full list”. That needs a decision before the privacy copy and store forms are final.
  5. Rent and pace nudges inside their own cards? Then the Home nudge card only carries renewals, old IOUs and treats.
  6. Share image footer. The domain isn’t settled (spec 11), so it says “Made with Senbud”. Is there a URL or handle to add?
  7. Are by-item splits and full Wrapped still Pro? Spec 8 says yes, and these frames lock them. The paywall lists only receipts, the unlimited assistant and subscription reminders.
