Questions people ask first.

Do I need to connect my bank?

No. You add spending by photo, by typing a line, or by tapping it in. Senbud never asks for a bank login.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start. Pro is restored through your Apple or Google account, so there’s nothing to sign up for. Sync between phones may come later, and signing in will only ever be for that.

Where is my data stored?

In the app, on your phone. Make a backup file any time, and restore it on a new phone.

What does the AI see?

Only a receipt photo you choose to snap, or a question you ask Bud with a short summary of your month. It answers and keeps nothing. AI can be turned off.

When can I get it?

Senbud is coming to iPhone and Android. Join the waitlist and you’ll get one email when it’s out.

Build notes

Responsiveness

  • 1100 px and up: two-column hero, Bud overlaps the phone’s left edge, three phones in a row.
  • Below 1100 px: hero stacks (headline, form, then phone), and Bud moves beside the phone.
  • 1060 px and down: the three app screens become a swipeable row with scroll snap, so each phone stays a readable size.
  • 900 px and down: receipt slips, privacy cards and the Bud/Friends splits go to one column; privacy arrows turn downward.
  • 760 px and down: nav keeps only the logo and the waitlist button; pricing cards stack; section padding drops from 96 to 64 px.
  • 420 px and down: hero phone scales to 74%, Bud to 76 px. Gutter never under 20 px and no sideways scrolling. Buttons and fields are 48 px tall; the small Yearly/Monthly switch is 44 px (Apple’s minimum).
  • Phones are drawn at real 375 pt and scaled with zoom, so the app screens stay pixel-true at every width.

Animation

  • One easing everywhere: cubic-bezier(.22,.68,.14,1), the app’s highlighter curve from tokens.ts.
  • On load, once: highlighter swipes under “safe to spend” (0.9 s), the phone rises 40 px (0.9 s), then Bud’s slip prints out line by line (1.1 s, stepped like a thermal printer) and the number counts up to $19.18.
  • Ambient, slow: Bud bobs every 5 s and blinks every 5.5 s; the treat jar’s surface ripples. Nothing else moves on its own.
  • On hover: waitlist buttons lift 2 px with a highlighter shine; cards lift 4 px; app phones tilt 1° toward you.
  • Pricing: the Yearly/Monthly switch swaps the price in place.
  • Reduced motion: every animation above is off; the page looks the same, just still. Content is never hidden waiting for a scroll trigger.
  • In Next.js this is plain CSS plus the existing MotionProvider; no new animation library.

Sleek details

  • Three cobalt bands only (hero, privacy, closing call), with paper and white bands between, so the page has a rhythm instead of ten look-alike cards.
  • One highlighter per section: the marked phrase in each heading. On dark paper it becomes yellow text instead of a stroke.
  • Real product, not illustrations: every phone is the actual app design from the screen concepts.
  • Receipts as the signature: torn edges and mono type for anything that is money (ways to add, Bud’s slips, the footer).
  • Type: system SF Pro, 800 weight, tight tracking (−3.5%) on headlines; body 17–20 px at 1.5 line height; prices in tabular figures.
  • Shape and depth: 20–24 px radius on cards, full pills for buttons; shadows only on things that float (phones, Bud, chat).
  • No emoji anywhere; light, dark and system themes all designed.
